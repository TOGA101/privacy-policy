# Toneify Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 26, 2025_

Toneify ("the App") provides AI-assisted color grading for user photos. This Privacy Policy explains how the App collects, uses, and protects information when you interact with it.

## Information We Collect
Toneify requires you to log in using your Google account to access the App's features. We collect and store the following personal information via Firebase Authentication:
- Username
- Email address
- User ID
- Remaining Credits
- Purchase History

To deliver the AI color grading service, the App also temporarily collects:
- The photo you choose to upload.
- The natural-language style description you provide to guide the color grading.

## How We Use Information
We use Firebase to authenticate users and store account-related data (username, email, user ID, credits, purchase history) to provide a personalized experience and manage your credits.

The App encrypts the uploaded photo and style description and transmits them to a Toneify processing service located in the United States. The server uses this information solely to generate a color-graded version of the photo and returns the result to the App. We do not use your data for analytics, advertising, or other secondary purposes.

## Data Storage and Retention
**Account Data:** Account data (Username, Email, User ID, Credits, Purchase Records) is stored securely using Firebase. This data is retained indefinitely to maintain your account status and purchase history, unless you request deletion.

**Image Data:** User photos and style descriptions are processed entirely in volatile memory on the server. The service does not write this information to persistent storage, databases, or logs. Once processing completes and the result is returned, the data is discarded.

## **ACCOUNT DELETION**
**You have full control over your data. You can delete your account and ALL associated data (including Username, Email, User ID, remaining Credits, and Purchase History) at any time through the following methods:**

1.  **In-App:** Click the **"Delete account"** button within the App settings. This will immediately delete all your data.
2.  **Email Request:** Contact us at **kaihuancai@gmail.com** to request deletion. We guarantee to process your request and delete all your data within **7 days**.

## Security
Toneify uses encrypted network connections to safeguard data in transit between the App and the Toneify server. We maintain technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect your content while it is being processed and your account data while stored.

## In-App Purchases
Toneify is distributed through the Google Play Store and offers in-app purchases. Payments are handled by Google Play Billing, and Toneify does not receive or store your payment credentials. We do store a record of your purchases associated with your account to manage your credits. Please review Google’s privacy policy for more information about how they handle transaction data.

## Children’s Privacy
Toneify is not directed to children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has submitted content through the App, please contact us so we can delete the content.

## International Data Transfers
Because Toneify’s processing servers are located in the United States, your photo and style description will be transferred to, processed in, and stored (temporarily in memory) in the United States regardless of your location. By using the App, you consent to this transfer.

## Your Rights and Choices
You have the right to access and delete your personal information. Please refer to the **ACCOUNT DELETION** section above for instructions on how to delete your account and data.

## Contact Us
If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or Toneify’s data practices, please contact us at kaihuancai@gmail.com.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If changes are made, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this policy. Continued use of the App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
