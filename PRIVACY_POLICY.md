# Toneify Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 04, 2025_

Toneify ("the App") provides AI-assisted color grading for user photos. This Privacy Policy explains how the App collects, uses, and protects information when you interact with it.

## Information We Collect
Toneify does not require account registration and does not collect personally identifiable information. To deliver the AI color grading service, the App temporarily collects the following data:

- The photo you choose to upload.
- The natural-language style description you provide to guide the color grading.

## How We Use Information
The App encrypts the uploaded photo and style description and transmits them to a Toneify processing service located in the United States. The server uses this information solely to generate a color-graded version of the photo and returns the result to the App. We do not use your data for analytics, advertising, or other secondary purposes.

## Data Storage and Retention
User photos and style descriptions are processed entirely in volatile memory on the server. The service does not write this information to persistent storage, databases, or logs. Once processing completes and the result is returned, the data is discarded. Toneify does not maintain user accounts, so there is no profile data or history retained.

## Security
Toneify uses encrypted network connections to safeguard data in transit between the App and the Toneify server. We maintain technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect your content while it is being processed.

## In-App Purchases
Toneify is distributed through the Google Play Store and offers in-app purchases. Payments are handled by Google Play Billing, and Toneify does not receive or store your payment credentials. Please review Google’s privacy policy for more information about how they handle transaction data.

## Children’s Privacy
Toneify is not directed to children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has submitted content through the App, please contact us so we can delete the content.

## International Data Transfers
Because Toneify’s processing servers are located in the United States, your photo and style description will be transferred to, processed in, and stored (temporarily in memory) in the United States regardless of your location. By using the App, you consent to this transfer.

## Your Rights and Choices
Since Toneify does not store personal data or maintain user accounts, there are limited rights or choices to exercise. You may withdraw consent for processing at any time by uninstalling the App or choosing not to upload content. If you have questions about your data, you can reach out using the contact information below.
